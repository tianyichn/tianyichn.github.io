Research

Our group tackles energy and nuclear challenges by expediting the development and deployment of advanced materials. These materials are crucial for the structural, functional, and fuel components of contemporary energy systems.

  • We advance scholarship at OSU and beyond by providing expertise in understanding the mechanisms of material behavior at the microstructural level, linking fabrication, processing, and service degradation to performance.
  • We integrate research with education, fostering hands-on experience and problem-solving through advanced materials techniques.
