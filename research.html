Research

We connect what happens inside a material’s microstructure to how the whole component performs, so that new nuclear and energy materials can be designed and qualified faster.

Our approach

  1. Observe. In-situ SEM experiments reveal which microstructural features and deformation processes matter.
  2. Measure. Micromechanical tests extract local properties and mechanisms.
  3. Model. Those measurements feed mechanistic models that predict performance with higher fidelity than empirical fits.
