Understanding materials performance and failure to accelerate qualification and deployment

At the Advanced Thermophysical and Operando Mechanics (ATOM) Lab, we combine electron microscopy, in-situ mechanical testing, and thermophysical analysis to show how materials behave under real operating conditions. We specialize in nuclear fuels and irradiated or radioactive materials, and bring the same instruments and rigor to metals, ceramics, and air-sensitive materials for energy, aerospace, and manufacturing.
